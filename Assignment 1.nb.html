Assignment 1

The Data

The data being used in this assignment is Motor Trend Car Road Tests (from the mtcars package). The data was extracted from the 1974 Motor Trend US magazine.

The data comprises fuel consumption and 10 aspects of automobile design and performance for 32 automobiles from the 1973-74 models.

The Motor Trend Car Road Tests data frame has 32 rows and 11 columns

Research Questions

  1. Is engine size related to gas mileage?

  2. Do automatic and manual transmission cars have different gas mileage?

Plots

mtcars |> 
  ggplot(aes(x = disp, y = mpg)) + 
  geom_point(color = "lightblue", size = 2, alpha = 0.75) + 
  geom_smooth(method = "lm", colour = "black") +
  labs(x = "Engine Size (cubic inches)",  
       y = "Miles per Gallon",
       title = "Relationship Between Engine Size and Gas Mileage")

mtcars |> 
  group_by(am) |> 
  summarise(mean_mpg = mean(mpg)) |> 
  ggplot(aes(y = mean_mpg, x = am)) +
  geom_point(color = "red",size = 2) + 
  labs(y = "Average Miles per Gallon",            x = "Transmission (0 = Automatic, 1 = Manual", 
      title = "Difference in Gas Milage for Automatic vs Manual Cars")

Conclusion

There was a difference in gas mileage for automatic and manual transmission cars and it was large. The average mileage of manual cars was about 7 mpg higher than automatic cars. There was also a strong relation between engine size and gas mileage, with cars that had larger engines tending to have lower gas mileage.
